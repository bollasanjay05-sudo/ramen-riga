RAMEN RIGA — ANIMATED WEBSITE
================================

HOW TO VIEW
1. Unzip the downloaded Ramen_Riga_Website.zip.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. The animated Mount Fuji and koi-fish graphics are included locally.
4. For the dish / gallery photos and fonts to display, connect to the internet.

FILES
- index.html           Complete structure and text
- style.css            Responsive styling and animated backgrounds
- script.js            Menu categories, UI interactions and scroll effects
- assets/fuji-transparent.png    Your supplied Mount Fuji image with transparent background
- assets/koi-orange.png          Your supplied orange koi extracted for animation
- assets/koi-ivory.png           Your supplied white koi extracted for animation
- assets/koi-duo.png             Combined koi image, optional
- assets/ramen-illustration.svg  Local illustrated image fallback
- assets/favicon.png             Browser icon

NOTES
- Wolt images are hot-linked from Wolt's servers; they could be blocked or changed. The website falls back to illustrated bowl tiles if needed. Restaurant/chef imagery uses remote URLs too.
- Instagram itself could not be directly accessed; three photos attributed to ramenriga on a third-party feature are used, and the chef portrait is from Wolt's interview.
- Ensure you have permission to reuse third-party photography before publishing a production site. For permanent hosting, replace linked images with licensed, locally hosted assets.
- Prices and the Wolt delivery hours reflect the publicly visible listing on November 6, 2026, not guaranteed current prices or restaurant dine-in hours.
- Ordering buttons lead to Wolt and do NOT create an order directly on this site.
- This is a front-end website; it does not include a payment processor, reservation backend or admin dashboard.
